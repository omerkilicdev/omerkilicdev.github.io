Research

Aresty Research Assistant · Flapping-Wing Aerodynamics, Rutgers MAE

2026 – Present

PI: Prof. Mitsunori Denda · Bio-Inspired Micro Aerial Vehicles · insectflight.rutgers.edu

  • Built a Fast Multipole Method in Julia for a discrete-vortex flapping-wing solver and dropped it into the group's MATLAB 3-D wake code as a replacement velocity kernel; with vectorized exact evaluation, cut a 15-step simulation from 1,783 s to 50 s.
