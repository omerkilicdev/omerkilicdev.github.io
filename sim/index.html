wake lab

This is not a recording. A flat plate is pitching and heaving in a free stream, shedding a point vortex from its trailing edge every instant, and the whole wake is convecting itself by Biot–Savart summation over every pair of vortices — live, in your browser, at every frame. Change the stroke and watch the street reorganise.

what you are watching

The plate’s motion sets an effective angle of attack; a quasi-steady Kutta condition turns its rate of change into shed circulation (red = positive, blue = negative). Each free vortex then moves with the velocity induced by all the others, evaluated with a Krasny-regularised Biot–Savart kernel. With N vortices that is N² interactions per step — the counter under the canvas is counting them. That quadratic sum is exactly what my research replaces: multipole moments and a fast multipole method collapse the far field so the same wake costs close to O(N), which is what cut a 15-step three-dimensional run from 1,783 s to 50 s. Try it: slow the frequency and the street spreads into gentle pairs; raise the heave at high frequency and the clusters roll up tight and launch a thrust jet — the reverse Kármán street that flapping flight lives on.
